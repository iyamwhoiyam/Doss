The adaptive application core

Your operation changes every quarter.
Your software should change with it.

Contract manufacturing does not fit a template. Every customer brings a new format, a new active, a new packaging spec, a new set of tests. Rigid ERP forces the plant to bend around the software. Enova AMP inverts that: a composable core your team reconfigures the same week the requirement lands — without a consultant, a change order, or a six-month implementation.

Three building blocks

Every module is built from the same three parts

Formulation, procurement, production, QA, costing, fulfillment — each is assembled from Formulas, Specs, and Runs. Learn three concepts, configure the whole plant.

Spreadsheet-like tables as your system of record

AMP uses live tables as the source of truth for every formula, item, lot, and order. Build views that span R&D, procurement, inventory and production, then edit inline with full permissions and audit history. A price change on one raw material reprices every affected formula and open quote in real time.

  • Versioned formulas. Every revision retained, diffable, and tied to the lots it produced.
  • Inline editing with permissions. QA approves, sales quotes, the floor executes — same row, different rights.
  • Cross-module views. Filter open POs by the formulas waiting on them.
  • Full audit trail. Who changed what, when, and why — 21 CFR Part 11 style e-signatures.

Specs turn inputs into actions

A customer RFQ, a raw material receipt, a deviation report — each is a spec. Pre-populate fields from the record, apply rules and validations at the point of entry, and trigger downstream steps the moment it is submitted. The compliance checks run before anyone can hit save, not after the batch is made.

  • Validation at entry. D3 upper limits, capsule shell capacity, Vitamin E IU→mg AT conversion.
  • Pre-filled from the record. Item codes, current pricing, and prior revisions load automatically.
  • Blocking and warning gates. CITES botanicals block; Prop 65 categories warn and attach testing.
  • Submit triggers work. One spec spawns the quote, the PO draft, and the QA task.

Runs automate the floor in real time

Configure workflows that route a job through the plant and fire on any event. Synced to your integrations and specs, a run can reserve inventory, schedule a room, open the batch record, hold the lot for test results, and release to shipping — with a human in the loop exactly where regulation requires one and nowhere it does not.

  • Event-driven. Any event can trigger any action: receipt, approval, test result, ship confirm.
  • Room and equipment scheduling. 17 production rooms, allocated against real capacity.
  • QA holds enforced in software. A lot cannot ship before its COA clears.
  • Exceptions escalate themselves. Deviations open a CAPA and notify the owner.

QualityStudio

The real-time BI layer for the plant

QualityStudio turns live operational data into insight without exporting anything. Yield by room, cost variance by formula, deviation rate by shift, on-time release by customer — built as dashboards over the same records the floor is writing to, so the number on the screen is the number in the batch record.

  • No separate BI stack. Dashboards are built on live tables, not a nightly export.
  • Margin visibility per run. Quoted COGS against actual, while the batch is still open.
  • Quality trending. Deviations, OOS results, and CAPA closure rates over time.
  • Share a view, not a screenshot. Permissions follow the underlying data.

Enovabot

An AI copilot that knows your formulary

Enovabot solves problems and makes operational changes through plain-language prompts — drafting a formula, repricing a tier, finding every lot that used a recalled raw material. It can create tables, modify records, and trigger runs, always with explicit approval and a restorable snapshot behind it.

  • Approval required. Every write is previewed as a diff before it commits.
  • Grounded in your data. Answers cite the item code, lot, or revision they came from.
  • Compliance-aware. It refuses to draft a formula that breaches a documented limit.
  • Reversible. Automatic snapshots before any bulk change.
